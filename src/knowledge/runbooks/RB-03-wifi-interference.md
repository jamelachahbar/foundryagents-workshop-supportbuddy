---
id: RB-03
title: Wi-Fi interference
symptoms: ["slow Wi-Fi", "Wi-Fi drops", "internet works on cable", "many neighbouring networks", "2.4 GHz congested"]
escalate_if: "Wi-Fi stays slow after the placement and band checks"
synthetic: true
---

1. Check whether the problem also happens on a device connected by cable. If the cable is fine, the problem is local Wi-Fi.
2. Move the modem to an open, central and raised spot, away from metal, mirrors and microwave ovens.
3. Use the 5 GHz network for devices close to the modem; it is less crowded than 2.4 GHz.
4. Keep 2.4 GHz for devices far from the modem; it reaches further but is slower.
5. If rooms far from the modem still have a weak signal, follow RB-11.
