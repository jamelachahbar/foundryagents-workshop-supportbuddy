---
id: RB-10
title: Cabling and wall socket checks
symptoms: ["loose cable", "damaged cable", "broadband light off after moving the modem"]
synthetic: true
---

1. Check the modem's power cable and plug.
2. Check the fibre or DSL cable between the wall socket and the modem is firmly connected at both ends.
3. Check visible cables for kinks or damage.
4. Do not open the wall socket or the modem.
5. If the line stays down after these checks, continue with RB-01.
