---
id: RB-06
title: Slow speed
symptoms: ["slow internet", "measured speed below subscription", "buffering", "downloads slow"]
synthetic: true
---

1. Compare the measured speed with the subscribed profile from the line status.
2. Ask whether the slowness happens on Wi-Fi, on cable, or both.
3. If the line delivers close to its subscribed speed, the slowness is local: follow RB-03 or RB-11.
4. If the line itself is far below its profile with errors or resyncs, follow RB-07.
