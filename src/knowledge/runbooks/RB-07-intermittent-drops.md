---
id: RB-07
title: Intermittent drops
symptoms: ["connection keeps dropping", "constant disconnections", "many resyncs", "high line errors"]
escalate_if: "repeated resyncs or high error counts without an area incident"
synthetic: true
---

1. Check the line's resync count and 24-hour error count.
2. Check known incidents for the postcode (RB-09).
3. Many resyncs or errors without an incident point to a line fault. The customer cannot fix this.
4. Stop self-service troubleshooting and hand the case over to a human advisor (RB-12).
5. Do not promise a technician visit or a repair time.
