---
id: RB-01
title: No sync / broadband light off
symptoms: ["broadband light off", "broadband light blinking", "no internet on every device"]
escalate_if: "line stays down after the power and cable checks"
synthetic: true
---

1. Confirm the modem has mains power and its power light is on.
2. Check the fibre or DSL cable is firmly seated at both ends (see RB-10).
3. Check whether every device is affected or only one device.
4. If the broadband or fibre light stays off, do not restart the modem repeatedly; check for a known area incident (RB-09).
5. If there is no incident and the line stays down, hand the case over to a human advisor with the line status.
