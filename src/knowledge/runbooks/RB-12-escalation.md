title: Escalation to a human advisor
symptoms: ["line fault", "repeated resyncs", "restart did not help", "evidence inconclusive", "customer asks for a person"]
synthetic: true
---

1. Use this procedure only when the investigation already decided to hand the case over. It does not apply when another runbook fixes the problem.
2. Confirm the identified customer and the affected line.
3. Summarise the evidence: line status, incidents checked, equipment check and what was already tried.
4. State clearly what could not be determined and what a human should check next.
5. Hand the case over and give the customer the handover reference.
6. Never promise a technician visit, a call-back time or a repair date.
