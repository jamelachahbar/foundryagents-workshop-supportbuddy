---
id: RB-09
title: Known area incident communication
symptoms: ["outage in customer area", "several customers affected", "open area incident", "line down"]
synthetic: true
---

1. Identify the customer and their postcode.
2. Check known incidents for the postcode or area.
3. Tell the customer only what the incident record says: what happened and the estimated restore time.
4. Do not suggest restarting the modem while an open incident affects the line; it will not help.
5. If the customer is still offline after the estimated restore time, hand the case over to a human advisor.
