---
id: RB-02
title: Modem restart procedure
symptoms: ["modem not responding", "modem unreachable", "all lights frozen", "no internet but line is up"]
applies_to: ["MockBox 4", "MockBox 5"]
escalate_if: "the modem is still unreachable after one restart"
synthetic: true
---

1. Use this procedure only when the line is up, there is no open area incident, and the modem does not answer the ping.
2. Ask the customer to switch the modem off at the power button or plug.
3. Wait 30 seconds, then switch it on again.
4. Wait about 3 minutes until the power and broadband lights are steady before testing a website.
5. Ask the customer to try again after the restart. Restart once only.
