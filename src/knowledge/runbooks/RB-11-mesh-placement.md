---
id: RB-11
title: Mesh / extender placement
symptoms: ["weak Wi-Fi in distant room", "Wi-Fi slow upstairs", "weak client signal"]
escalate_if: "coverage stays weak after placement changes"
synthetic: true
---

1. Confirm the weak coverage is on Wi-Fi only and the cable connection is fine.
2. Place the modem centrally and raised, away from thick walls, metal and large appliances.
3. For distant rooms, a mesh point or Wi-Fi extender placed halfway between the modem and the room helps most.
4. Do not place an extender in the room with the weak signal; it needs a good signal itself.
5. If coverage stays weak after these changes, hand the case over to a human advisor.
