---
id: RB-04
title: Fibre outage / loss of signal
symptoms: ["red LOS light", "loss of signal", "fibre line down", "all devices offline"]
escalate_if: "loss of signal persists and no area incident explains it"
synthetic: true
---

1. Confirm whether the line shows loss of signal.
2. Check the fibre cable is connected and not visibly bent or damaged.
3. Never bend, unplug repeatedly or clean the fibre connector.
4. Check known incidents for the customer's postcode (RB-09). An open incident explains the outage.
5. Without an incident, hand the case over to a human advisor for a network or field check.
